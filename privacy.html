Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

TL;DR

PhoneYaps is a fully local iOS app. It does not track you, collect analytics, or sell any data. All audio triggers and custom sounds are processed entirely on your device. The only network activity is the App Store handling your purchase. This website does not collect any data from visitors.

1. Information We Do Not Collect (App)

The PhoneYaps app does not collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • System events (battery level, WiFi status, Bluetooth connections, etc.)
  • Audio files you import (custom sounds)
  • Crash reports
  • Location or device identifiers

There are no analytics libraries, tracking pixels, or third-party trackers in the app.

2. On-Device Processing

All triggers (low battery, charger events, WiFi changes, Bluetooth connect/disconnect, etc.) are detected and processed locally on your iPhone. No event data ever leaves your device.

3. Custom Sounds & User Content

When you import your own audio files:

  • All sounds stay on your iPhone.
  • PhoneYaps does not upload, scan, or transmit your custom audio files.
  • You are solely responsible for ensuring any custom sounds you add do not infringe copyrights or other rights.

4. Local Storage

PhoneYaps stores your preferences (enabled triggers, selected voice packs, volume, custom sound mappings, etc.) locally using iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your device.

5. Network Requests

PhoneYaps makes no network requests of its own. Purchase and license management is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We have no visibility into your purchase details beyond what Apple provides to developers.

6. Permissions

PhoneYaps requests access to:

  • Notifications — to remind you to reopen the app if iOS suspends it in the background.
  • Bluetooth — to detect when Bluetooth devices connect or disconnect (required by iOS even for passive monitoring).
  • Audio output — to play sounds.

It does not access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, health data, or any other sensitive data.

7. Background Execution

iOS limits background execution for apps not in specific system categories. PhoneYaps requests the audio background mode to stay alive while backgrounded. If iOS suspends the app, it schedules a local notification to remind you to reopen it — this notification is scheduled locally on your device and no data is sent to any server.

8. Third-Party Services

  • App Store — Apple handles all purchase and payment processing. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Voice clips — Pre-made voices were generated with ElevenLabs. All audio is bundled locally in the app; there is no runtime connection to ElevenLabs.

9. Children's Privacy

PhoneYaps does not knowingly collect data from anyone. Since the app collects no personal data, this section is largely not applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Continued use of PhoneYaps after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.

11. Contact

Questions? Reach out to support@hackem.app
